# Explicit consent Implementation Plan

> Execute inline in the existing isolated commercial-pilot worktree. Continuation authorized by the owner's «ok sigamos» after the contact update; uses the existing legal-consent proposal as design. No deployment is included.

**Goal:** Record two explicit decisions for account use and each client's business, and enforce them before new commercial processing while preserving history, cancellation and rights.

**Architecture:** Versioned shared document text; private immutable receipts and revisioned state in existing `serverSettings` documents. Existing Database transactions serialize revocation with booking creation. A short-lived process-signed anonymous preauthorization captures account decisions before Google without storing anonymous database records; authenticated claiming binds it once to a UID. Session reads no longer create profiles. Missing, stale, revoked or corrupt consent fails closed for new operations, never inventing acceptance for existing users.

**Tech Stack:** Existing Next/React/TypeScript, Node crypto, Zod, Database D1/Firestore; no new dependencies or provider calls.

**Spec:** [Legal review and consent design](../../product/2026-10-16-legal-consent-review.md), including its unresolved publication decisions. The current continuation authorizes local implementation; texts remain local drafts until operational/legal decisions are closed.

## Global Constraints

- No production mutation, inference calls, invitation issuance, secret changes or migration.
- Separate authentication from consent; users can sign in to inspect/cancel existing appointments and exercise rights without granting new purposes.
- Account consent is neutral for clients and owners; business acceptance includes the named business/contact and IA notice. No invented business policy or provider promises.
- Preserve existing booking fingerprints, durable replay, paid quotas, rates, logos and public projections. No chat bodies/IP in receipts/logs.
- Scope is account (UID) and business_client (UID + business ID). No implicit migration of old profiles.
- Store document snapshots, hashes, affirmative choices, server times and versioned purposes; immutable event replay cannot reactivate revoked authorization.
- Preauthorization is valid 20 minutes and may be repeated after process restart; it stores no identity until authenticated claiming. A consumed token cannot authorize another UID or a new acceptance after revocation.
- Retention, provider contracts, business legal identities and sensitive/minor data remain publication decisions; do not declare compliance complete.

## Review Focus

- Auth token callback races or identity changes cannot attach a prior user's acceptance to the next user (task 3).
- Revocation concurrent with booking commits serializes through the consent-state read (task 2).
- Old acceptance request replay cannot overwrite revocation or increment revisions (task 1).
- No missing/stale receipt can consume AI quota or call inference, including queue waits and direct-domain calls (task 2).
- Existing history/cancel/recovery remains usable after revocation; no background batch or contact submission after local revocation (tasks 2–3).
